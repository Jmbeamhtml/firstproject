Have you ever wondered what to put on your toast in the morning? Do you like Peanut Butter? Can you tolerate Syrup? If you said yes to these questions, then I have the perfect, easy recepie for you!

Making it

First start by placing your bread of choce in the toaster and turning the toaster on preferred setting. Next grab a plate and set next to toaster. Grab your toast from the toaster when it is ready and place on the plate. Open your peanute butter and grab a butter knife. Spread prefferd amount of peanut butter on slice of toast.then squirt heavy amount of syrup on top.Conradulations! You made it!
